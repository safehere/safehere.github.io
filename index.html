🌿 Who we are

SafeHere exists to meet people in their places of need—online, in the streets, wherever they are—and to point them to the Shepherd, with the same heart of compassion Jesus had for the harassed and helpless.

“When he saw the crowds, he had compassion on them, because they were harassed and helpless, like sheep without a shepherd.” — Matthew 9:36 🙏

💛 What we do

We offer the comfort and living hope we have received in Christ so that others in any affliction can find refuge and hope in him. We aim to be salt and light—a visible, tangible presence that preserves and illuminates—so that good works and clear pathways point to the Father and bring him glory.

🌟 Our vision

We envision a world where:

  • The harassed and helpless are seen and met with compassion, through clear pathways from first contact to the gospel, to community, and to disciples who make disciples.
  • People in affliction find comfort and living hope, so that no one who responds is left without a spiritual home or the hope of Christ.
  • The body of Christ is visibly salt and light, shining before others so they see good works and glorify the Father.

We are sensitive first to the lost, the hopeless, and the seeking. We envision every such person finding a clear path to the Shepherd; every new believer connected to community; and discipleship happening in community, to the glory of God. 🙌
