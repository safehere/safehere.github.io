About SafeHere

Our guiding passages, vision, mission, and leadership

Guiding Passages

Matthew 9:35–36

"And Jesus went throughout all the cities and villages, teaching in their synagogues and proclaiming the gospel of the kingdom and healing every disease and every affliction. When he saw the crowds, he had compassion on them, because they were harassed and helpless, like sheep without a shepherd." — Matthew 9:35–36

Jesus goes to where people are—towns and villages—and responds with compassion to the harassed and helpless. SafeHere exists to meet people in their places of need (including online and in the streets) and to point them to the Shepherd, with the same heart of compassion and the same readiness for a harvest that is ready.

2 Corinthians 1:3–4

"Blessed be the God and Father of our Lord Jesus Christ, the Father of mercies and God of all comfort, who comforts us in all our affliction, so that we may be able to comfort those who are in any affliction, with the comfort with which we ourselves are comforted by God." — 2 Corinthians 1:3–4

God comforts us so we can comfort others. Many today are in affliction and have lost hope. SafeHere is a channel of that comfort: we offer the Living Hope we have received in Christ so that others in any affliction can find refuge and hope in him.

Matthew 5:13–16

"You are the salt of the earth. … You are the light of the world. … In the same way, let your light shine before others, so that they may see your good works and give glory to your Father who is in heaven." — Matthew 5:13–16

We are called to be salt and light—visible, tangible presence that preserves and illuminates. SafeHere aims to show the love and truth of Christ in ways people can see and experience, so that good works and clear pathways point to the Father and bring him glory.

Vision

A safe place for the lost, especially for the hopeless and those at the edge of life, where they come to know Jesus Christ the Living Hope, find a home in His church, and grow as disciples who make disciples, all to the glory of God.

Mission

SafeHere provides an online safe space that connects the lost with registered Christian counselors and reaches seekers through online evangelism, pointing them to Jesus Christ the Living Hope and inviting them to register so we can connect them to the nearest church or small-group discipler.
